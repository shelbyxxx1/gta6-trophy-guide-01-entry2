    How this guide was built

    • The game released 19 November 2026. The trophy list is the official 55 as published on PlayStation Network, via Exophase & PSNProfiles; the counts (42 shooting-range challenges, 14 drone-race locations, 18 meditation spots) come from the same list.
    • Five trophies had no confirmed unlock method at launch — In The Beginning…, Capital Offense, Personal Best, Bad Luck, I Guess, The Truth Is Out There and Ten Pin. Their guides say so plainly instead of inventing a method.
    • Only one trophy is flagged possibly missable: Positive Mental Attitude, whose first meditation spot is offered in Chapter 1 and never again. Everything else is earnable in free-roam after the story.
    • There is no mission replay and no New Game Plus, so the roadmap tells you to keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one before the final mission.
    • Your progress never leaves this browser: it is saved in localStorage, and export/import moves it as a file you own.
